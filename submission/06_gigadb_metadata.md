## GigaDB dataset metadata

**Dataset title:**
Supporting data for "Cross-cohort reproducibility and transportability of
the taxonomic dysbiosis signature of inflammatory bowel disease"

**Abstract:**
Re-analysis of three publicly available 16S rRNA metagenomic cohorts of
inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) — Nielsen et al. 2014 (n=318), Ijaz et al.
2017 (n=50), and HMP2/IBDMDB (n=130; 498 total including 197 IBD and 301
non-IBD controls) — using a fixed-seed, fully reproducible pipeline.
Differential abundance was estimated with ANCOM-BC2 and pooled with
random-effects meta-analysis (metafor REML); taxonomic dysbiosis signatures
were benchmarked by nested leave-one-cohort-out random-forest
classification. This dataset contains the cohort manifests, per-cohort
differential-abundance coefficient tables, pooled meta-analysis effect
tables, replication scorecards (species and genus level), held-out-sample
predictions, and ROC/AUC summary tables that underlie every figure and
statistic in the manuscript, together with the figure files and this README.
